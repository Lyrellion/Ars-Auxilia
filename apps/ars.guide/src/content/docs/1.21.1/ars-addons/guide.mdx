## Ars Nouveau 1.21.1 Addon Guide

### Ars Essentials
*“Must have” addons.*

**Ars Elemental** - adds elemental gear, additional spell glyphs, new archwood biomes, and magical creatures

**Ars Additions** - adds Ars structures to the world, plus a number of new quality of life items

**Not Enough Glyphs** - adds additional glyphs and the spell binder

### Ars Expansions
*Addons that add new content not linked to a specific non-Ars mod.*

**Starbunclemania** - Adds additional options for automation and the ability to convert source to a liquid.

**Ars Controle** - Adds Blocks, Items, and Glyphs focused on giving you more control

**Ars Fauna** - adds variant Ars creatures to the world

**Ars Artillery** - adds deployable defense turrets that can be crafted

**Ars Caelum** - adds additional floating island rituals to create a wider variety of biome islands

**Ars Structurize** - adds a variety of Ars themed structures

**Ars Affinity** - adds school-based perk trees

**Ars Zero** - adds a caster staff and new glyphs

**Ars Trinkets** - adds a leveling system and configurable stat boosts

**Ars Ingressum** - adds a ritual for traveling to other dimensions

**Ars Musique** - adds music disc crafting and buffs

**Ars Astrologique** - adds new content themed around space

**More Arsnouveau** - adds a few new glyphs

**Ars Nouveau Combat Glyphs** - adds two new glyphs specifically for combat

**Ars Prism (WIP)** - reworks mechanics into multiple types of magic based on a series of new types of source gems

**Adam's Ars Plus (WIP)** - adds high end content, including powerful new glyphs and bosses

### Ars Equipment
*Addons that focus on adding new Ars gear.*

**All the Arcanist Gear** - Adds additional tiers of gear designed for “All the Mods” modpacks.

**Ars Elemancy** - Extension for Ars Elemental that adds dual and quad elemental gear.

**Ars Nouveau: Greatest Discount** - adds higher tiers of mana-related accessories

**Ars Violetgold Equipment** - adds new tier of stronger equipment beyond netherite

### Ars Functionality
*Addons that tweak functionality without adding significant new content.*

**Ars Unification** - adds glyph functionality with processes from other mods

**Ars Polymorphia** - adds recipe selection support to the Lectern

**Ars Lumos** - adds glowing effect to Archwood and Source

**Ars Numeric HUD** - shows mana numbers

**Put Mana In** - lets you manually charge source jars

**Ars Deco** - adds dyeable variants of many Ars blocks

**Ars Planarium Eternal Day** - changes the skybox of the planarium to daytime

**Ars Nouveau: Two-Way Portals** - adds a new type of warp scroll that creates a two way portal and support for Immersive Portals

**Ars Nouveau: Chapters** - lets you create new tabs in a spellbook, allowing for more spells

**Crafting Terminal Improvements** - modifies the lectern GUI

**Custom Ars Portals** - allows for irregularly shaped ars warp portals

### Unrecommended Addons
*These mods are made to work with Ars Nouveau, but are not widely recognized in the Ars community.*

**Simple Ars Nouveau Starter Kit** - starts players out with a spellbook and Arcanist Hat

**Ars Repairing** - enhanced version of the Repairing Thread (unnecessary with current version)

**Ars Spellbook Rebalance** - changes the Spellbook recipe

**Ender Source Jars** - adds ender source jars with multiple channels (makes warp relays obsolete)

**Ars Janky ME Source Compat** - changes to Ars Energistique ME Source Jar

**Ars Compressed** - adds compressed source gem blocks

**Ars Dragongate** - locks Ars content behind the Ender Dragon

**Liquid Source** - adds liquid source (just use Starbunclemania)

**Make Ars Lectern Great Again** - modifies the lectern GUI (use Crafting Terminal Improvements instead)

**Ars Arcane Matrix** - adds a source generator and ore generator multiblock to Ars Nouveau

### Mana Unification/Conversion Mods
*These mods are designed to unify or convert mana pools between mods. This is HIGHLY discouraged, as it can very quickly create balance issues due to differences in how mods handle mana usage.*

**Ars &lt;-&gt; Iron Mana Bridge** - merges mana pools

**Ars 'n Spells** - compatibilty between Ars and Iron's Spells

**Ars Technic** (not to be confused with Ars Technica) - adds RF&lt;-&gt;mana conversion

### Incompatible Addons
*These mods are made to work with Ars Nouveau, but cause crashes or directly break base mod functionality in some way.*

**Create Ars Compact** - adds AN recipes to Create (breaks recipes) **MODRINTH only**

**ArsThread Expansion** - allows non Ars armors to have threads added (unneeded with Ars Additions and breaks recipes)

**Ars Meteorites** - summon meteorites using magic (causes crash)

**Dynamic Trees - Ars Nouveau** (Dynamic Trees) (causes instability)

**Better Storage Lectern** - breaks scrolling on lectern

**Vminus** - technically NOT an Ars addon, but manages to break Ars recipes
